A blockchain that's already running

These people already share one ledger. Nobody owns it, and anyone can look at it. This is what it looks like right now.

Step 1 · Find the blockchain · in plain words

A blockchain has no head office, so there is no sign-up page to hunt for. To find one, you need two things.

1. An entry point. The address of someone already on the network. Here, it is this website. Bitcoin's software has a built-in list of "seed" computers to ask first.

2. A way to know it's the real one. Every blockchain starts with a first block, the genesis block. Its fingerprint is the chain's ID, and every honest copy begins with the same one.

Anyone can look at the chain without joining, just like you are doing now. Joining is what lets you take part.

Step 2 · in plain words

Nobody gives you an account. You make your own, and it is a pair of keys.

Private key: your secret signature stamp. Only you have it.

Public key: lets everyone check your stamp is real, but not copy it.

Address: your account number, made from your public key.

3Download and check the chain

Steps 3 and 4 · in plain words

Step 3, download: ask the network for every block, from the very first one, and keep your own full copy.

Step 4, check: don't trust whoever sent it. Check every block yourself with four questions:

  1. Fingerprint: does the block's hash match its contents?
  2. Link: does it point to the block before it?
  3. Work: does its hash start with four zeros, proving the puzzle was solved?
  4. Signatures: is every payment signed by its sender, who had enough coins?

Step 5: you're a member. Take part.

Step 5 · Take part · in plain words

You are a full member now. There are three things you can do.

Receive: a member signs a payment to your address. It becomes final once a block includes it.

Mine: solve a small puzzle: find a number so the block's fingerprint starts with four zeros. The winner adds the next block and earns 50 coins, and everyone's copy updates.

Send: sign a payment with your private key. Every member checks your signature before accepting it.
